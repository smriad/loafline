## What I Built

Loafline is a **permission-to-loaf engine**: paste or record a messy commitment, and open-source AI tells you if you can safely defer it, the smallest closing action, and when it's safe until — with a copy-paste **permission slip** and a **lazy payoff** timer judges can verify.
